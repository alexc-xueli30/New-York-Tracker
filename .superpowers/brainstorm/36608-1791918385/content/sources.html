Which NYC news sources should feed SPOTTED?

All of these have free RSS feeds — no API keys, no auth. Pick as many as you want.

A

Gothamist

NYC's most-read local news site. Covers everything — transit, housing, politics, infrastructure. High volume, very relevant. gothamist.com/feed

B

City Limits

Investigative journalism focused on poverty, housing, and city services — exactly what 311 data tracks. Lower volume but high signal. citylimits.org/feed

C

Streetsblog NYC

Potholes, street lights, transit, sidewalks — the closest thematic match to the 311 complaint types we track. Infrastructure journalism. nyc.streetsblog.org/feed

D

The City

Non-profit investigative outlet covering city government and neighborhoods. Strong on housing, sanitation, and public services. thecity.nyc/feed

You can select multiple — they'll all be merged and sorted by publish date in the unified feed.
