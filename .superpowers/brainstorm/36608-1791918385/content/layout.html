How should SPOTTED be laid out?

You want community photos + NYC civic news feed. Three ways to present both together.

A

Sub-tabs inside SPOTTED

Two inner tabs: PHOTOS and FEED. User switches between them. Clean separation — photos get their own full-screen grid, news gets its own scrollable list. Most common pattern.

B

Unified scroll — photos + news mixed

One feed where community photos and news articles are interleaved by recency. A photo from yesterday, a news article from this morning, another photo — all in one timeline. Feels like a social feed.

C

Split view — photos left, news right

On desktop: photo grid on the left half, news feed on the right. On mobile: stacks vertically (photos then news). Both visible at once on wide screens.
