Where do community photos come from?

311 reports don't include photos. Three ways to get them into the feed.

A

User upload (needs Supabase setup)

Add a camera button in the feed — tap to snap a photo of a pothole, broken light, etc. Photo + caption + location stored in Supabase. Shows up in the unified feed next to news articles. Requires setting up a real Supabase project (currently using placeholder URL). Most powerful, most work.

Pros

  • Real civic participation
  • Visual evidence of issues
  • Tied to specific locations

Cons

  • Need Supabase configured
  • Moderation challenge
  • Storage costs
B

Skip photos — news feed only for now

Launch with the NYC civic news feed (Gothamist, City Limits, WNYC RSS) — zero setup, works today. Add community photo upload in a future version once Supabase is configured. The unified feed just shows news cards in the meantime.

Pros

  • Works immediately, no backend
  • RSS is free forever
  • Clean scope

Cons

  • No community participation yet
  • Less "spotted" feeling
C

311 report cards as "sightings" (no real photos)

Instead of photos, show 311 complaint cards in the feed — same data as the map but formatted as a vertical timeline card with severity badge, address, borough, and a colored severity banner where a photo would be. News articles interleaved. No extra setup needed.

Pros

  • Uses data already loaded
  • No Supabase needed
  • Looks like a real feed

Cons

  • No real community photos
  • Redundant with the map
