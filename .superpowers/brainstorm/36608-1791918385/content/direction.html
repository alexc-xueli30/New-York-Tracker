What should SPOTTED be for?

The current TikTok embed shows Mamdani's general feed. Before picking a platform, let's nail down what this tab is actually trying to do.

A

Mamdani's own posts

Show Zohran's social content — campaign updates, NYC issue videos, statements. Think of it as his broadcast channel embedded in the tracker. Best if: the app is for his campaign followers.

B

Community-submitted photos of NYC issues

People who see a problem take a photo and submit it here — pothole on 5th Ave, broken streetlight, overflowing trash. Stored in Supabase, displayed in a photo grid. Best if: the app is a civic participation tool.

C

Live NYC civic news / social feed

Aggregate public posts about NYC civic issues — pull from RSS feeds (Gothamist, City Limits, NYC.gov), hashtag content, or news APIs. Curated, auto-updating. Best if: the app is a real-time city dashboard.
